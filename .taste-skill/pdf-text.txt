What is Shellmates
Shellmates is a student club at the National Higher School of 
Computer Science in Algiers (ESI). Founded in 2011 under the 
name OWASP, our club specializes in the field of 
Cybersecurity. We aim to promote cybersecurity awareness 
and culture in Algeria by organizing various events 
throughout the year.
Our Objectives
01
Create a space for knowledge sharing and 
constructive competition.
02
Introduce those interested to the fundamentals 
of cybersecurity.
03 Aim to gain international recognition.

Club Departments
HR 
 Human Resources: Manages the club's members and 
internal organization. HR handles recruitment, member 
integration, team coordination, follow-up, and maintaining a

===== PAGE 10 =====
Club Activities
Shellmates follows a hands-on, workshop-driven approach to 
cybersecurity education. At the beginning of each season, we introduce 
members to the fundamentals of cybersecurity, followed by specialized 
workshops covering various offensive security fields, including Web 
Pentesting, Cryptography, Pwn, Reverse Engineering, and more. Beyond 
learning, we put knowledge into practice through internal and external 
events. Internal events are designed exclusively for members, providing 
a space to apply acquired skills through practical challenges and team-
based activities. Our external events are open to a wider audience and 
focus more strongly on competition, challenges, and showcasing 
cybersecurity skills. We also organize Hack The Box meetups, where 
members collaborate, solve challenges, and learn from one another in a 
practical environment. Alongside our technical activities, we host 
internal recreational activities that strengthen team spirit and foster 
connections within the community. Finally, each department has the 
opportunity to organize specialized workshops and activities related to 
its own field, allowing members to develop a broader range of technical, 
creative, organizational, and professional skills.

===== PAGE 17 =====
Conclusion
Shellmates is more than a cybersecurity club. It is a 
community built around learning, experimentation, 
competition, and collaboration.
From discovering the fundamentals of cybersecurity 
to exploring advanced fields, participating in 
challenges, building projects, organizing events, and 
working alongside others, every member has the 
opportunity to learn, contribute, and grow.
Whether your goal is to deepen your technical skills, 
explore a new field, develop professional 
experience, or simply meet people who share the 
same passion, there is a place for you at Shellmates.
Learn. Build. Compete. Connect.
Welcome to Shellmates.
